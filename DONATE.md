Dinge schaffe.

**Du musst nicht spenden.** Meine Software bleibt kostenlos, egal ob du
spendest oder nicht. Aber wenn dir gefällt, was ich mache, und du mir eine
Freude machen willst — dann spende, was du willst. Jeder Euro hilft.

## 🇬🇧 English

### My software is hobby & donationware

My programs (e.g. **SysPilot**) are hobby projects of mine — free, open
source, no ads, no telemetry. If you like them and they help you, I'd
appreciate a voluntary donation: **"If you like it, donate what you want."**

**What the money goes to:** Honestly — pizza, döner, coffee, rolling tobacco,
and if enough comes in: VS Code with Copilot and maybe a self-hosted AI
(uncensored/abliterated/heretic) to experiment with.

**Why I'm asking at all:** I'm unable to work due to health reasons and live
in a care facility with high costs — my financial situation is rather tight.
I build software because I enjoy it and like making useful things.

**You don't have to donate.** My software stays free, whether you donate or
not. But if you like what I do and want to make my day — donate whatever you
want. Every euro helps.
